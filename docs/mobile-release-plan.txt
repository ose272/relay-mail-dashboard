Sendar release status - 27 November 2026

Implemented in this release:
- Password signup/signin route directly to the original responsive dashboard.
- Google identity login is separate from optional Gmail mailbox authorization.
- New customer leads, plans, settings and connection metadata are workspace-scoped.
- Mailbox disconnect clears saved tokens and attempts provider revocation.
- Installable web-app manifest, supplied logo and generic offline page. API data and authenticated HTML are never cached for offline viewing.
- The known test account is removed separately through a narrowly scoped operator action, preserving recent pseudonymous sending usage.

Not a full public mail launch:
- Customer campaign queues, background sending, inbox sync, reply actions and tracking are not connected yet. The existing administrator mail engine is intentionally not exposed to customers.
- The dashboard marks these capabilities unavailable rather than showing mock campaigns or dispatching under another customer's identity.
- Public app description, privacy policy, terms, support.sendar@gmail.com contact and a self-service account-deletion flow are included. Customer mail functionality and Google review remain release gates.
- Google restricted Gmail access requires its own approval; publishing status is not verification.

Mobile distribution:
- No signed Android App Bundle or iOS archive has been built or uploaded.
- Owner has no Apple Developer or Google Play Console accounts yet. Enrollment, identity checks, app records and store review remain external requirements.
- Android needs a final package ID, app signing setup, native Google OAuth client or a verified browser-based flow, and test distribution.
- New personal Google Play accounts generally require 12 opted-in closed testers for 14 continuous days before applying for production access.
- iOS needs a Mac/Xcode build environment, bundle ID, signing and TestFlight. Review must consider Apple's minimum-functionality and login-services requirements. Google login may require an equivalent privacy-preserving login option unless an exception applies.
- A simple website wrapper is not a promise of Apple approval. Native navigation, external-browser OAuth and useful platform integration must be tested.
- App privacy/Data safety declarations must match actual data processing. Future subscriptions require a separate review of store billing rules; Paystack checkout remains inactive.
