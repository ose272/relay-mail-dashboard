Relay Mail

About Relay Mail

Relay Mail is an independent email outreach and conversation dashboard. It is not Google or Gmail and is not affiliated with Google.

Your account password

Create a separate Relay Mail password when you register. Our sign-in pages ask for that password, not your Google or Gmail password. The administrator sign-in uses the workspace's own dashboard password.

Mailbox connections

Creating a Relay Mail account does not connect a Gmail mailbox. Google OAuth connection is not yet available for new customer accounts. The existing administrator workspace supports separately authorized mailbox connections. A Google app password is a separate credential; it is not your normal Google account password.

Data and access

Your setup profile is separate from other customers. Creating an account does not grant access to the existing administrator's mailbox or campaigns. Connected workspaces handle account details, leads, messages and delivery records for the features their owner uses.

Browser security warnings

If your browser shows a dangerous-site warning, stop and report the address and warning to the workspace administrator. Do not enter credentials or bypass the warning.
